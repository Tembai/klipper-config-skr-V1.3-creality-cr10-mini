## Inspect these mechanical points

Power the printer off first.

Check:

* The probe body cannot move inside the new bracket.
* The entire hotend carriage has no rocking movement.
* The bed does not rock on its wheels.
* The probe pin is straight and slides freely.
* The probe cable is not pulling on the probe when Z moves.
* The probe connector pins are fully seated.
* The Z lead-screw coupler screws are tight.
* The X gantry does not move vertically when the nozzle is gently pressed.
* The probe pin repeatedly drops to the same visible position.

Do not casually tighten the screw on top of the probe. Klipper notes that an incorrectly positioned top screw or displaced magnetic core can prevent reliable pin movement; adjustment is appropriate only when deployment is visibly sticky or inconsistent. ([Klipper Documentation][2])
